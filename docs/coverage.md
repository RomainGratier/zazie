# Draft provider profile and coverage

The included `eu-ai-act-provider-starter` profile, version `0.1.0`, is a **draft operational starter**. It is intended for customers that have already determined their high-risk classification, role and applicable obligations. Zazie does not classify a system or provide a complete legal compliance assessment.

The profile offers four evidence workflows:

| Workflow             | Operational support                                               | Source mapping                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Risk management      | Risks, mitigations and residual-risk review                       | [Article 9](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-9), paragraphs 1–2 and 5                                                                                                  |
| Evaluation evidence  | Defined criteria, measurements and version-bound evidence         | [Article 9](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-9), paragraphs 6 and 8; [Article 15](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-15), paragraphs 1 and 3–5 |
| Human oversight      | Adopted procedures, practical intervention evidence and follow-up | [Article 14](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-14), paragraphs 1–4                                                                                                      |
| Monitoring follow-up | Monitoring ownership, operational events and assigned findings    | [Article 72](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-72), paragraphs 1–3; amendment review pending                                                                            |

Official sources were retrieved on 23 September 2026. The Service Desk identifies its displayed provisions as the original June 2024 text, and Article 72 expressly warns that its text has not yet been updated for amendments. The [Commission's AI Act overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) points to the later amendment. Retrieval is recorded separately from legal review. This release does not claim to have reconciled the complete consolidated legislation, transition arrangements or subsequent guidance, and it does not calculate statutory deadlines.

The release-review and freshness rules are product controls chosen by the organisation; they are not assertions that every named step is legally mandatory. An authorised internal approval records a decision against specific evidence. It does not create an EU declaration, certificate or permission from a regulator.

The starter leaves classification, prohibited-practice assessment, full data governance, complete technical documentation, the full quality-management system, deployer instructions, legal retention decisions, conformity assessment, registration, and sector/deployer-specific duties to further work. Dossiers show these omissions. In particular, the product does not assume every private employer has the same fundamental-rights impact-assessment duties.

Procedure templates are drafts until the organisation adapts and adopts a version. Neither an adopted document nor an uploaded report proves that a real-world safeguard works. Record operational evidence, its limitations and the responsible review.

To revise a pack, create a new content version with changed sources, applicability inputs, interpretation status and coverage. Existing release snapshots retain their earlier version. Do not silently edit the interpretation attached to a historical approval.
