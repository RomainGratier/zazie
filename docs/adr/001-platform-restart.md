# Zazie platform restart

The 0.2 platform replaces the research prototype preserved at commit `0f3a3bb`.
Historical reports are not validation of this platform. The MIT licence,
repository, and attribution remain unchanged.

Customers submit already-classified systems and externally produced evaluation
measurements. No model service is required. Human approval binds immutable evidence
and explicit criteria. The first product implements milestones 1–3 of the September
2026 brief, with a draft provider starter profile and visible omissions.

An explicitly authorised reviewer may approve their own submission. Administrator
permission alone is insufficient. This is an internal release decision.
